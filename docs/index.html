Labelscope

Find the words you care about on ingredient labels.

Add the words you want to watch for — "palm oil", "red 40", "whey" — then point your camera at a label. Labelscope reads the text on-device and highlights any match, along with common alternative names for it. Nothing you scan leaves your phone.

Labelscope is a convenience aid, not a substitute for reading labels yourself. Text recognition can misread small print and the dictionary of alternative names is not exhaustive; always double-check the label.
